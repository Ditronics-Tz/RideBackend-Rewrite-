# Mainland Tanzania document policy

Checked 12 October 2026. Assumption: Mainland Tanzania private-hire/ride-hailing; Zanzibar and individual vehicle categories need their own confirmation.

| Upload | Rida policy | Legal evidence / distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Driving licence | Required, expiry required | LATRA's operator guide requires driver licence details. A document review does not validate the licence against an issuing authority. |
| Vehicle card (kadi ya gari) | Required | Regulation 5(1)(c) lists the registration card for a private-hire vehicle licence application. |
| National ID | Required as Rida identity evidence | Regulation 5(1)(b) requires citizenship evidence for individual applicants; this does not establish NIDA as the only acceptable evidence. |
| Insurance | Optional **upload**, expiry required if provided | Motor Vehicles Insurance Act, sections 4–5, requires compliant third-party cover/security for ordinary road use and addresses fare-paying passengers. Optional upload does not make insurance legally optional. |
| Vehicle photo | Required by Rida | No universal statutory requirement to upload this particular photo to Rida was established in the sources reviewed. |

The principal private-hire regulations additionally require vehicle roadworthiness evidence, a service-provider licence and driver authorization. These are not collected by the current four-document checklist, so an approved Rida profile is **not a complete legal compliance certification**. Extend the policy for those permits before using it as the sole operating-compliance control. The 2026 amendment changes licence categories, fees and ride-sharing conditions; read it with the principal regulations.
